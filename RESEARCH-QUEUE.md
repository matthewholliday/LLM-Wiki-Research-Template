# Research Queue

[Wiki index](INDEX.md)

Add questions under Open. File order determines which open question is researched next. Each entry uses a stable `RQ-0001` style ID and records its question, added date, and status.

Statuses are `open`, `in-progress`, `blocked`, and `done`. Unfinished entries stay under Open with notes about remaining work. Completed entries move in full to Done with their completion date, outcome, and links to wiki results. Done remains the last section.
