Hello Cursor Team and Community,

I have been exploring **Cursor – The AI Code Editor**, and I see that it has many powerful features. However, as a blind programmer using screen readers like **NVDA and JAWS**, I have encountered significant accessibility issues that make it difficult to benefit from these features.

One major issue is that **I cannot read the suggestions provided by the AI or programming language extensions.** In contrast, **VSCode handles this seamlessly** because of its strong commitment to accessibility.

I would like to ask: **Does Cursor have an interest in improving accessibility?**

If accessibility improvements are possible, I would be happy to provide detailed explanations of the issues faced by screen reader users.

Looking forward to your response!

**Best regards,**
